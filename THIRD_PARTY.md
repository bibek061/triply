# Third-party data and integration references

- **Place facts:** Facility and measurement handling follows OpenStreetMap documentation for [campsites](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Tag:tourism%3Dcamp_site), [SAC hiking difficulty](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Key:sac_scale), and [route distance](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Key:distance). Missing/ambiguous tags remain unknown; maps do not establish current access or conditions. Traveler uploads are labeled with their author and are not presented as Commons-licensed images.

- **Country catalog:** [mledoze/countries](https://github.com/mledoze/countries), licensed under the Open Database License 1.0. The adapted `data/countries.json` database is provided under ODbL 1.0; see `licenses/countries-ODbL.txt`. Triply selects country names, codes, regions, capitals and coordinates, and replaces currency mappings with current CLDR tender periods. Source URLs and retrieval time are embedded in the snapshot. The app serves the adapted database and license text.
- **Currency mapping:** [Unicode CLDR JSON](https://github.com/unicode-org/cldr-json). Copyright © 2015–2024 Unicode, Inc. Distributed under Unicode License V3, included in `licenses/unicode.txt`. Current tender periods are evaluated at sync time; refresh the snapshot to reflect future changes.
- **Places and geocoding:** © [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), ODbL. Data is requested through [Nominatim](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/nominatim/) and [Overpass](https://dev.overpass-api.de/overpass-doc/en/preface/commons.html). Source map links and attribution are displayed beside results. Public endpoints are development conveniences, not a service-level agreement; configure your own suitable service for public scale.
- **Exchange rates:** [ExchangeRate-API Open Access](https://www.exchangerate-api.com/docs/free), with required “Rates By Exchange Rate API” attribution in the converter. The raw rate feed is cached privately and is not served or checked into Git; the endpoint only returns the requested conversion.
- **Pricing adapter references:** [Duffel v2](https://duffel.com/docs/api/v2/offer-requests) and [Booking.com Demand API v3.2](https://developers.booking.com/demand/docs/open-api/3.2/demand-api). Triply uses original native-fetch adapters, with no SDK bundled. Account access, production approval, usage limits and applicable provider terms remain the owner's responsibility. The old Amadeus self-service adapter was replaced after its official repositories were archived.
- **Booking.com property photos:** Images returned by the approved account's accommodations/details API are linked from validated bstatic.com hosts and labeled as provider property photos. Their use is governed by the account's Booking.com agreement; they are not represented as Creative Commons images.
- **Photography and fonts:** Inspiration images are loaded from Unsplash and do not verify the appearance of a named property or place. DM Sans and Manrope are loaded through Google Fonts with local system font fallbacks. Community photos are user uploads.
- **Provider links:** Google Travel, Booking.com, Expedia, KAYAK, Rentalcars.com, and DiscoverCars are third-party services. Triply has no claimed partnership and does not copy their live inventory or complete reservations.
- **Destination suggestions:** © [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Adapted from [cities15000.zip and admin1CodesASCII.txt](https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/): selected columns, numeric coordinates/population, and known country codes. Source and retrieval time are embedded in `data/destinations.json`. Country labels use the country catalog above.
- **Map library:** [Leaflet 1.9.4](https://leafletjs.com), BSD-2-Clause, vendored with its full notice in `vendor/leaflet/LICENSE`. Map tiles © OpenStreetMap contributors. Refresh the pinned library with `node scripts/vendor-map.js`.
- **Place photography:** Explicit OpenStreetMap Wikimedia Commons file tags, Wikidata P18 claims and tagged Wikipedia article images provide direct reference matches. The fallback uses the [Commons geosearch API](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:API/MediaWiki#Retrieve_files_given_a_pair_of_coordinates_(latitude,_longitude)), requiring both a meaningful place-name match and coordinates within 150 m. Nearby photos are labeled with distance; contributor metadata may be incomplete or inaccurate. Commons file pages, photographer credits and individual licenses accompany each image. Embedded images are limited to CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC0 or Public domain metadata, with cropping disclosed. No unrelated image replaces a missing match. Photos remain remote; Triply does not redistribute an image dataset or guarantee coverage of every place.
- **Google Images links:** Place cards offer public search links for further browsing. Google/social-network images are not scraped or imported. These links do not grant rights to republish the search results.
- **City-card photography:** Country/state browsing uses Wikipedia city article photos after checking article or linked Wikidata coordinates against the GeoNames city center (50 km maximum). Country codes are checked when supplied. Name variants and region/country qualifiers distinguish similarly named cities. Commons author, license and source attribution are retained, with the same license restrictions as place photography.
- **Provider/inspiration photography:** Free stock photographs found using Pexafy, sourced from [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/license/) and [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/license). Seven asset records retain their source page, photographer, and license in `data/photo-fallbacks.json`. The flight/car/stay photos remain on provider comparison cards with attribution. Category photos are no longer used as fallback images for named places.
