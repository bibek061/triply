# Third-party data and integration references

- **Country catalog:** [mledoze/countries](https://github.com/mledoze/countries), licensed under the Open Database License 1.0. The adapted `data/countries.json` database is provided under ODbL 1.0; see `licenses/countries-ODbL.txt`. Triply selects country names, codes, regions, capitals and coordinates, and replaces currency mappings with current CLDR tender periods. Source URLs and retrieval time are embedded in the snapshot. The app serves the adapted database and license text.
- **Currency mapping:** [Unicode CLDR JSON](https://github.com/unicode-org/cldr-json). Copyright © 2015–2024 Unicode, Inc. Distributed under Unicode License V3, included in `licenses/unicode.txt`. Current tender periods are evaluated at sync time; refresh the snapshot to reflect future changes.
- **Places and geocoding:** © [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), ODbL. Data is requested through [Nominatim](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/nominatim/) and [Overpass](https://dev.overpass-api.de/overpass-doc/en/preface/commons.html). Source map links and attribution are displayed beside results. Public endpoints are development conveniences, not a service-level agreement; configure your own suitable service for public scale.
- **Exchange rates:** [ExchangeRate-API Open Access](https://www.exchangerate-api.com/docs/free), with required “Rates By Exchange Rate API” attribution in the converter. The raw rate feed is cached privately and is not served or checked into Git; the endpoint only returns the requested conversion.
- **Flight adapter references:** [Official Amadeus Node SDK](https://github.com/amadeus4dev/amadeus-node) and [Flight Offers Search specification](https://github.com/amadeus4dev/amadeus-open-api-specification). Triply contains its own small native-fetch adapter. No SDK package is bundled. Credentials, account permissions, production approval, and service terms remain the owner's responsibility.
- **Photography and fonts:** Inspiration images are loaded from Unsplash and do not verify the appearance of a named property or place. DM Sans and Manrope are loaded through Google Fonts with local system font fallbacks. Community photos are user uploads.
- **Provider links:** Google Travel, Booking.com, Expedia, KAYAK, Rentalcars.com, and DiscoverCars are third-party services. Triply has no claimed partnership and does not copy their live inventory or complete reservations.
